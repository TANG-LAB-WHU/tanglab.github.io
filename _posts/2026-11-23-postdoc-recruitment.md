The Joint Research Group of **Prof. Jinjun Li** and **Prof. Siqi Tang** at the School of Resource and Environmental Sciences, Wuhan University, is inviting applications from domestic and international candidates for multiple full-time **Postdoctoral Researcher** positions.

Focusing on application-oriented fundamental **AI4Science** research for high-value energy/resource utilization and negative-carbon cycles, the open positions span five core directions:

1. **Innovative Phosphogypsum Valorization** — phase-transition kinetics, selective impurity removal, and multiphase crystal growth modeling;
2. **Fischer-Tropsch Synthesis (FTS) Catalysis** — cost-effective, anti-coking, poison-resistant catalysts and multiscale reactor engineering;
3. **MLIP-AIMD & Autonomous AI Agents for CCUS** — physics-intrinsic MLIPs, interfacial CO₂ mineralization dynamics, and inverse material design;
4. **Scalable MLIP-AIMD for Advanced Activated Carbon & VOCs Recovery** — million-atom carbon network evolution and low-energy capture/resource recovery;
5. **Uncertainty Quantification-Driven Sustainable Engineering Design** — Bayesian inference, GSA, and dynamic stochastic LCA/TEA.

Competitive pre-tax annual packages range from **200,000 to 580,000 RMB** (including Hongyi Postdoctoral Fellowship / National Postdoctoral Program for Innovative Talents, Key-Funded, and Full/Project-Funded tiers), together with dedicated GPU/CPU HPC clusters, experimental platforms, and full Wuhan University benefits.
